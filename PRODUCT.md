# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners, landlords, property managers, and small commercial operators in Macon and nearby Middle Georgia communities who need a major appliance repaired, installed, bought, sold, or supported by practical handyman work. Visitors are often on a phone, dealing with a broken appliance, and need a fast way to understand fit, call, or book an estimate.

## Product Purpose

Turn local search traffic into qualified phone calls and Calendly bookings while establishing Appliance King as a clear, credible Macon appliance-repair entity. Success means visitors can immediately identify the services offered, confirm the normal coverage area, understand what happens after booking, and reach the business without friction.

## Brand Personality

Confident, mechanical, dependable. The voice is direct and helpful, with the calm urgency of a well-run service bay. It never exaggerates, invents proof, or hides important limits.

## Anti-references

- Generic contractor templates built from identical icon cards
- Black-and-gold “royal” clichés
- Cream-and-slate AI landing pages
- Fake review counters, invented years, or unsupported guarantees
- Decorative glassmorphism, gradient text, and motion that delays content
- Stock-photo-heavy pages that imply work Appliance King did not perform

## Design Principles

1. Answer the broken-appliance question first.
2. Make calling and booking equally obvious without becoming pushy.
3. Turn the real logo and service process into the visual world.
4. Show scope precisely; never substitute broad claims for trust.
5. Design for a one-handed mobile visit under time pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain visible focus, logical reading order, 44px touch targets, high contrast, keyboard-operable navigation, useful alt text, and complete reduced-motion behavior. Content remains visible without JavaScript-driven reveal effects.
